Calculation Questions — Formula + When To Use It
Q6 — % by Mass
% = (mass of solute / mass of solution) × 100
Use when: given grams of solute and grams of solvent/solution, asked for percentage concentration
Mass of solution = mass of solute + mass of solvent
Q7 — Molarity
M = moles / liters
Use when: given grams of a substance dissolved into a volume of solution
Always convert grams → moles using molar mass first, and mL → L
Q8 — Dilution
M₁V₁ = M₂V₂
Use when: you have a concentrated stock solution and need to make a less concentrated one
You'll be given 3 of the 4 values, solve for the missing one
Q19 — Arrhenius Definition

Acid= produces H⁺ in water
Base= produces OH⁻ in water

Q21 — Weak vs Strong Acid
No formula — conceptual
Use when: comparing pH of weak vs strong acid at same concentration
Weak acid = less ionization = fewer H⁺ ions = HIGHER pH than strong acid
Q22 — pH of HOCl (weak acid)
pH = −log[H⁺] pOH = −log[OH⁻] pH + pOH = 14
Use when: given molarity of an acid, asked for pH
For STRONG acids → molarity = [H⁺] directly
For WEAK acids → pH will be higher than −log(molarity)
Q23 — pH of KOH (base)
pOH = −log[OH⁻] pH = 14 − pOH
Use when: given molarity of a BASE, asked for pH
For strong bases → molarity = [OH⁻] → find pOH first → then subtract from 14
Q24 — Concentration of HBr from pH
[H⁺] = 10^(−pH)
Use when: given a pH and asked for the molarity of a STRONG ACID
Since HBr is a strong acid → molarity = [H⁺] = 10^(−pH)
Q25 — Concentration of NaOH from pH
pOH = 14 − pH [OH⁻] = 10^(−pOH)
Use when: given a pH and asked for the molarity of a STRONG BASE
Convert pH → pOH first, then find [OH⁻]
Q26 — Titration (finding unknown concentration)
M₁V₁ = M₂V₂
Use when: given volume and molarity of one solution + volume of another solution, asked for the unknown molarity
Same formula as dilution! The difference is context — here you're neutralizing acid with base
Q27 — Titration Lab (buret reading)
Total Volume = Final reading − Initial reading Then → M₁V₁ = M₂V₂
Use when: given buret readings and one concentration, asked to find concentration of the other solution
Step 1 always: subtract buret readings to get actual volume used
Step 2: plug into M₁V₁ = M₂V₂ and solve
